      Your information

      Know what stays here—and what leaves.

      Here is how this prototype handles information during your visit. It is a plain-language product notice, not a substitute for a reviewed privacy policy.

      01

      In this browser tab

      Tuition, budget, savings, manual account labels and balances, quote entries, calculations, and activity stay in page memory. They clear when you reload or close the tab; the planner does not save them to an account.

      02

      Online requests from this page

      Loading or refreshing rates calls this site’s rate endpoint, which retrieves Bangladesh Bank reference data. When you submit a general AI question, the browser sends only that question to this site’s assistant endpoint; that service also sends its instructions to CampusBridge AI. Your planner fields and quotes are not sent.

      03

      When you open a provider or export

      Provider websites have their own privacy practices. Google Fonts also receives a routine browser request for font assets, not your planner entries. A downloaded report is a local text file containing entered financial details and quote information; review it carefully before sharing or storing it.

      Prototype limitation: Student support contact details and formal privacy/terms documents are not configured in this build. Review these before offering the service to students. Do not enter account credentials, one-time codes, or identifying details in the planner or assistant.

      04 · What-if + take it with you

      Explore a different plan.

      Try alternate savings and monthly contribution assumptions. The scenario is a local preview until you apply it.

      Change the values to preview the impact.

      What will the downloaded report contain?

      It can include your corridor, tuition, savings, emergency fund, committed funds, income, living costs, funding gap, rate source and timestamp, and any eligible quote details. The report is generated in your browser as a text file; check it before sharing.

      Technical integration readiness
      From prototype to production

      Real integrations need real partners.

      To activate account data or payment features, the team must select providers for the actual student corridor and complete each provider’s onboarding, security, consent, and regulatory requirements. API credentials alone do not establish permission to offer a financial service.

      01

      Confirm countries and account providers

      Bank connectivity depends on the origin and destination, available regulated aggregators, provider coverage, and your organization’s eligibility.

      02

      Establish regulated payment access

      For cross-border tuition, identify the authorized bank or licensed payment partner that can legally serve the student and corridor.

      03

      Configure server-side credentials

      OAuth secrets and provider keys belong in a secured backend secret manager. They must never be pasted into this page or exposed to browsers.

      04

      Implement consent, verification, and audit

      Students must see the provider, requested data, purpose, retention, revocation path, and transaction details before authorizing access or payment.

      05

      Validate live data and operational controls

      Use provider production certification, server-side validation, monitoring, privacy review, security testing, and failure handling before launch.

      Product principles

      Trust is part of the interface.

      The experience is designed to distinguish a verified connection from a setup step, and a provider response from an estimate.

      01 · Provenance

      Show where data came from

      Every live response should include its provider, retrieval time, relevant currency direction, and limitations.

      02 · Consent

      Keep students in control

      Use provider-hosted authorization where available. Never ask users to enter banking passwords or one-time codes into CampusBridge.

      03 · No false signals

      Fail closed when disconnected

      When a connection is missing or stale, show the unavailable state. Do not substitute a fabricated rate, balance, offer, or transaction.
