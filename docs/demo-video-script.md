# CampusBridge walkthrough (about 3 minutes)

**0:00–0:25 — Scope**  
“CampusBridge helps Bangladesh-based students plan tuition and compare payment quotes. It does not supply exchange rates or fabricate provider offers. No direct payment connection is configured.”

**0:25–1:10 — Enter current student data**  
Choose Bangladesh → UK, enter tuition and the student's actual financial figures, then enter the exchange rate from a current bank or provider quote with its source and timestamp. Explain that the result is only as current and accurate as those inputs.

**1:10–1:45 — Plan and what-if**  
Calculate the funding gap, protected reserve, monthly target, and deadline projection. Adjust scenario savings and monthly savings, preview the change, then apply or reset it.

**1:45–2:25 — Real quote handoff**  
Open the university's official payment link (or the Convera/Flywire portal when directed by the institution). Request a quote for a specific principal and enter the actual total debit, recipient amount, delivery estimate, timestamp, and quote reference. Compare by total cost, recipient amount, or delivery time. Explain that CampusBridge does not validate or execute the quote.

**2:25–2:50 — Assistant and report**  
Ask about the funding gap or entered quotes. Show the local tool/activity entry and download the report. Explain that profile values are not sent to a model or provider by CampusBridge.

**2:50–3:00 — Limitations**  
“Provider support depends on the student's university and payment origin. The team must obtain provider approval and server API access before adding live quotes or payment functions.”
