# FinTechathon 2026 International Track checklist

## Verified from an official partner's published International Track page

The [IEEE Computational Intelligence Society Singapore Chapter's 2026 FinTechathon page](https://www.ieee-cis-singapore.org/2026-fintechathon) states:

- The International Track theme is “AI as a Financial Participant”; Topic C is Cross-border & Student Finance Assistant.
- Participants must be full-time students at universities outside Mainland China, Hong Kong SAR, Macao SAR, and Chinese Taipei.
- Teams have 2–5 members; at least 60% of a team must be non-Chinese nationals; each participant may join only one team.
- English is the official International Track language.
- The published schedule gives a registration and submission deadline of 20 October 2026 and identifies schedule times as Singapore Time (UTC+8).
- The page says to obtain the official registration form from the official competition website and send it to `fintechathon@ican-x.com` with subject format `International Track Registration – [Lead Country] – [Team Name] – [Team Leader’s Full Name]`. It says organizers aim to reply within 48 hours.

These facts are transcribed from that official-partner page; confirm they remain current through the [official competition website](https://fintechathon.g-ican.com/) and organizer communications.
