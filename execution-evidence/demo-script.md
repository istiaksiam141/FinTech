# Demo Video Script (4 minutes 30 seconds)

This is a script for a future recording, not evidence that a video exists. Use a preview or public app only with permission, synthetic data, and no real account details. The sample output below matches the 20 October 2026 browser run; current public rates and calculated figures may differ on another date.

| Time | On-screen actions and narration |

## Recording checklist

- Keep the finished video at five minutes or less.
- Use only fictional amounts and no real names, credentials, account identifiers, or private messages.
- If a current official rate appears, show its source and dates and say it is not guaranteed.
- Do not show a fabricated quote as if it came from Convera, Flywire, a bank, or another provider.
- Do not claim a sandbox, payment, real-user isolation, or production configuration was verified unless separate evidence supports it.
