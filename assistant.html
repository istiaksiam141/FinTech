Full data and privacy details

When a valid plan is available in this browser, each AI request includes a compact summary of its calculated costs, available and usable savings, funding gap, contribution dates, exchange-rate source and timestamp, and any matching savings-tracker totals or eligible entered quote comparisons. Full form fields, account details, tracker notes, and quote references are not sent. The AI explains the supplied results; the deterministic finance engine remains authoritative. Tracker deposits and quotes are student-entered and unverified. For a saved conversation, checking the option below also sends up to 11 recent messages from that same chat (up to 6,000 characters total); the server verifies your account and selected conversation before loading them. Signed-in conversations, including your questions and answers, are saved to your CampusBridge account. Do not include your name, student ID, account details, or other sensitive information.
