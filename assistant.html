AI assistant

Ask a general question. If you ask about a saved funding gap, the answer is calculated locally from your plan.

Before you askSupported planning questions are answered locally. For saved conversations, prior messages remain available in your account but are not sent to the AI service. Only the current question is sent. The server verifies the selected conversation and latest question using your signed-in Supabase session. Saved plans, balances, quotes, and other database records are not sent. Signed-out chats send only the current question. Signed-in conversations, including your questions and answers, are saved to your CampusBridge account. Do not include your name, student ID, account details, or other sensitive information. Your financial form and quote entries are not included.
