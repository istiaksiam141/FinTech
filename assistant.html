AI assistant

Ask a general question. If you ask about a saved funding gap, the answer is calculated locally from your plan.

Before you askSupported planning questions are answered locally. By default, only the current question is sent to the AI service. For a saved conversation, checking the option below sends up to 11 recent messages from that same chat (up to 6,000 characters total) to Groq as context for your answer; those messages may contain personal or financial details. The server verifies your account and selected conversation before loading them. Saved plans, balances, quotes, and other database records are not sent. Signed-out chats send only the current question. Signed-in conversations, including your questions and answers, are saved to your CampusBridge account. Do not include your name, student ID, account details, or other sensitive information. Your financial form and quote entries are not included.
